Things to ask AI

One way to use AI effectively is to ask it to explain a topic in a way that is easier to understand.
You can also ask it to create additional practice problems similar to the ones you are working on in class.
This can help you improve your understanding and prepare for tests and quizzes.
Another useful way to use AI is to have it review your writing and correct grammar and spelling mistakes.
You can ask it to explain how to use commas, exclamation marks, semicolons, and dashes correctly.
AI can also help you improve your sentence structure, expand your ideas, and make your paragraphs more organized.
If you do not understand a correction, ask AI to explain why it made the change so you can learn from your mistakes.
Remember, AI is a tool to help you learn, practice, and improve your skills, not just to give you answers.
